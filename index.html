Wiom TV Banner — Options v3

Ranked C > F > D > E > B > A. No television sets in graphics — the experience is TV but it plays on the phone, so visuals are phones + play buttons. Copy keeps the word "TV".
OBJECTIVE
At the payment-success moment, make every Wiom Net user aware that a free TV service is included with their connection — 75+ live channels, on the phone in their hand, at no extra cost. The banner's job is awareness, not conversion: a few-second glance should leave one sentence behind — "Wiom पर फ्री TV भी मिलता है।" Interested users can tap and try; for everyone else, knowing the service exists is the win.

Design implications: the three message atoms — TV · फ्री · इसी फोन पर — must land without reading small text; no urgency/scarcity mechanics; tap-through is a secondary metric.
Judged on: ① क्या ग्राहक समझेगा कि फोन पर TV जैसा अनुभव मिलेगा? ② क्या उसे दिखेगा और भरोसा होगा कि यह फ्री है?
